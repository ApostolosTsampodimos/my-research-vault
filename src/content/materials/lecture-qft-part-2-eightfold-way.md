abstract: >
  This is the second part of a short lecture series, given together with my
  fellow PhD student Cheng Cheng, introducing the basic concepts needed to catch
  a glimpse of QCD. Starting from the “particle zoo” of the mid-twentieth
  century, it introduces the basics of Lie algebras in particle physics (from SU(2) and
  isospin to the addition of states) and presents the eightfold way.

abstract: >
  Disformal transformations relate the gravitational metric to the metric that
  matter feels. Acting on spinors, a general disformal transformation shifts the
  kinetic term, introduces an axial term and rescales the mass. When the
  transformation is sourced by ultralight dark matter, these terms map onto
  coefficients of the Standard-Model Extension, so the dark matter shows up as
  apparent Lorentz violation, which particle-physics data can bound.

abstract: >
  Every measurement is, at its core, a comparison between quantities coincident in
  space and time; the local unit of length need therefore carry no intrinsic physical
  content. We render this redundancy explicit by extending the Hamiltonian phase space
  of general relativity with a compensating scale field, whereupon local rescalings
  become a gauge symmetry that leaves the algebra of observables unaltered. A change of
  conformal frame thus ceases to be a field redefinition and becomes a gauge
  transformation, and the choice of frame reduces to the choice of a clock that measures
  scales. Complete observables are invariant under this choice: an expanding universe
  with fixed masses and a static universe with growing masses yield identical measured
  redshifts, for example. Brans–Dicke theory of gravity serves as an illustrative case.

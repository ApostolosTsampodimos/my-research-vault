title: "Hamiltonian analysis of disformally coupled fermions in scalar-tensor theories"
date: 2026-10-12

authors: ["Alexander Ganz", "Apostolos Tsampodimos"]

abstract: >
  We study disformal transformations of N scalar fields coupled to a Dirac fermion. The
  resulting axial coupling generically introduces higher derivatives in the scalar field
  equations of motion; for field-dependent free functions, these are absent if and only if
  the disformal transformation acts along a single field-space direction. We show that these
  higher derivatives do not signal an Ostrogradsky ghost. After the transformation, the
  fermion registers the derivatives of the matter-frame tetrad only through the axial
  current, so that every coupling to the derivatives of the scalar fields multiplies a
  fermion bilinear, which is Grassmann even and nilpotent. Such terms do not contribute to
  the body of the constraint matrix, and at each level of the Grassmann expansion the field
  equations are linear, with the principal part of the theory without fermions. Through a
  Hamiltonian analysis, we show that the number of degrees of freedom is that of the bosonic
  theory plus that of the fermion, both in the Einstein frame, for free functions depending
  on the scalar fields and their kinetic matrix, and for minimally coupled fermions in
  multi-field DHOST theories. The same mechanism applies to disformal transformations with
  higher derivatives of a single scalar field, for which fermions impose no condition beyond
  those required by bosonic matter.
tags: ["disformal", "fermions", "Hamiltonian analysis", "DHOST", "Ostrogradsky"]
